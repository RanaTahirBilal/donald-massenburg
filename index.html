About

Care built on questions and answers

I am part of the medical team at Prevea St. Mary's Health Center in Green Bay, Wisconsin, where I provide evaluation and treatment for a wide range of inflammatory and autoimmune conditions.

My approach is relational. Good rheumatology starts with listening, so I take the time to ask questions, answer them, and build a working relationship with each patient.

Conditions

What I treat

I provide comprehensive evaluation and treatment for inflammatory and autoimmune conditions, including:

  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Juvenile idiopathic arthritis
  • Psoriasis and psoriatic arthritis
  • Uveitis and other inflammatory eye disorders
  • Lupus
  • Gout
  • Polymyalgia rheumatica
  • Ankylosing spondylitis
  • Polymyositis and dermatomyositis
  • Sarcoidosis
  • Scleroderma
